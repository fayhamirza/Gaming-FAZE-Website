Call of Duty is a gem of a game that lets you extract your rage. Anytime I’m left with feelings of exhaustion and anger from a long day, COD helps me shake it off and let loose by taking out my pent-up stress on the battlefield. With exclusive maps and top-notch equipment, this game allows you to have an A class experience. Although, when it comes to the gameplay and rankings, I can’t help but channel my inner angry teenage boy energy. The players sneak up on you and catch you off guard which takes all of my life’s worth of maturity to keep my phone in my hand and not thrown across the room. But that’s what makes the game fun and challenging, especially when playing it with your friends. With that being said, COD’s new and improve season is a MUST-PLAY!

machoman789

I don’t know what it is about Mario Kart, but every time I play it, it feels like so comforting. Whether I’m racing solo or going head-to-head with friends, it never fails to turn into total chaos — and I love it. There’s just something magical about drifting around a corner, snagging a lucky item box, and somehow pulling off a last-second win. I’ve played pretty much every version at this point — from the old-school days on the SNES to Mario Kart 8 Deluxe on the Switch. And every time, it’s the same mix of fun, frustration, and pure joy. It’s not just about winning; it’s about yelling at your friends in good fun, dodging banana peels, and praying your controller doesn't fly out of your hands. Oh and don’t get me started on the absolute betrayal of when your friend sabotages your win by throwing a shell at you just as you’re about to cross the finish line :( Honestly, Mario Kart isn’t just a racing game. It’s a tradition. It’s sleepovers, family battles, and inside jokes that somehow revolve around Yoshi or Rainbow Road. If you've ever played, you get it. And if you haven’t — what are you doing? Get in the kart.

mimi2468

Purble palace is a game that connects a lot of us to our childhood days. Released in 2007 and available on windows that became everyone’s go-to entertainment source. It is a very immersive game that consists of three simple yet interesting games. One of them is called “Cake Master” which is a cake baking game where players play the role of a talented baker and can use their creative skills in creating all sorts of fun designs on cakes and even prepare orders. Another one is a memory game called “Memory Match” this game tests the players with their memory skills. Players are required to match cards that are identical which they have to complete within a time constraint not to mention that the intensity of the game increases as the players progress through more levels and excel further in the game. Then there is “Fashion Frenzy” a game where players can let their imagination run free as they create all sorts of outfits, perfect for all the children that want to be fashion designers when the grow up as it allows them to embrace versatile fashion styles while also aiming to achieve the highest scores possible. There is no doubt that the diversity of the games that is provided in Purble Palace keep players fascinated and caught up for hours on end.
